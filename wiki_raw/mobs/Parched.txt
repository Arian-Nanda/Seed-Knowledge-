A '''parched''' is a [[skeleton]] variant that spawns in [[desert]]s. Parched behave similar to skeletons, but have less [[health]], do not burn in [[sun]]light, fire their [[bow]]s more slowly, take freeze damage, cannot convert to [[stray]]s, and shoot [[tipped arrow]]s of [[Weakness]].

== Spawning ==
Parched can [[Mob spawning|spawn]] in [[desert]]s at night, replacing some [[skeleton]]s.

{{IN|java}}, parched spawn directly under the sky in groups of 4 in place of 50% of all skeletons spawned in desert biomes.

{{IN|bedrock}}, parched spawn on the surface in groups of 2-4 in place of {{frac|1|3}} (~33%) of all skeletons spawned in desert biomes. 

{{Mob spawn table}}

=== Camel husk jockeys ===
When a [[husk]] spawns [[spawn cycle|naturally]] and spawns in a space that does not block the collision box required for a [[camel husk]], it has a 10% chance to spawn as a [[camel husk jockey]], i.e. riding a camel husk, and spawning a parched as the second passenger.

=== Spider jockeys ===
{{exclusive|Bedrock|section=1}}[[Spider]]s and [[cave spider]]s spawned in deserts have a 1% chance to spawn as a [[spider jockey]], with an 80% chance of the rider being a parched.

== Behavior ==
Parched mostly behave like normal [[skeleton]]s except that they shoot extended [[arrows of Weakness]] instead of normal arrows, have their cooldown between each [[bow]] shot longer than normal skeletons at every 3.5 seconds on Easy and Normal and every 2.5 seconds on Hard, and they do not burn in sunlight. Parched are also immune to the [[Weakness]] effect, although this typically makes no effective difference since parched attack using projectiles, which are themselves not affected by Weakness.

In {{BE}}, like normal skeletons, [[stray]]s and [[bogged]], parched also do not retaliate against attacks from [[breeze]]s, despite them not spawning at the [[trial chambers]]. Breezes also don't retaliate against attacks from parched.

{{IN|be}}, the eyes of parched are [[emissive]] when [[Vibrant Visuals]] is enabled.

Being an [[undead]] mob, they are: 
* Damaged by the status effect [[Instant Health]] and healed by the status effect [[Instant Damage]].
* Unaffected by the status effects [[Regeneration]] and [[Poison]].
* Ignored by the [[wither]].
* Affected by the [[Smite]] [[enchantment]].
* Unable to swim in [[water]], but do not drown.
* A threat to [[armadillo]]s, causing them to hide in their shell.

Because they are under the "skeletons" [[entity type tag]]{{only|java}}, if a parched kills a [[creeper]], the creeper drops a [[music disc]], randomly selected from [[13]], [[Music Disc cat|cat]], [[Music Disc blocks|blocks]], [[chirp]], [[far]], [[mall]], [[mellohi]], [[stal]], [[strad]], [[ward]], [[11]], and [[wait]]. They also run away from wild and tamed wolves similar to other skeleton variants.

Similar to [[bogged]], parched take freezing [[damage]] and do not convert into [[stray]]s.<ref>{{bug|MC-268579||Bogged in powder snow freeze on death instead of converting to Stray|WAI}}</ref> Unlike [[husk]]s converting into zombies, parched do not turn into skeletons when submerged in [[water]] for too long.

=== Picking up items ===
{{main|Geared mobs#Picking up gear}}
Some parched spawn with the ability to pick up dropped [[item]]s from the ground. Unlike [[zombie]]s, however, they pick up only weapons, [[shield]]s, and armor. {{IN|Java}}, parched pick up items that they happen to walk near. {{IN|Bedrock}}, they actively search for and walk towards dropped items.

If the picked up item is a [[weapon]] (other than a bow), the parched uses the item as a melee weapon. Parched do not pick up [[spear]]s under any circumstances. {{IN|Java}}, parched prefer bows over other weapons, and do not pick up other weapons while wielding a bow. {{IN|Bedrock}}, the only weapon a parched will swap its held bow for is a [[sword]]. When picking up weapons, parched prefer ones that deal more damage ''without considering enchantments''. They also prefer weapons with NBT data over those without, and weapons that are less damaged.

If the picked up item is a shield, the parched equips the shield into their offhand. It cannot use the shield to block incoming attacks, however.

If the picked up item is [[armor]], a [[carved pumpkin]] or a [[head]], the parched equips itself with the item. Parched prefer armor that give more armor points, and will replace their current armor with it. Parched pick up cosmetic wearables (heads and carved pumpkins) as long as they aren't wearing a [[helmet]], and will replace the cosmetic wearable if they find a helmet.

If a parched has equipment and picks up something they prefer more, the parched drops the old equipment, with the same chance as if killed with Looting I{{Verify|Is this still the case in both Java and Bedrock?}}, in favor of the new one.

A parched equipped with a picked-up [[item]] becomes persistent and does not [[despawn]], similar to being [[Name Tag|named]].
