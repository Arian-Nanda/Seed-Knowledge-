A '''zombie horse''' was an unused, [[undead]] variant of the [[horse]]. In the [[Mounts of Mayhem]] update, the zombie horse gets a revamped version of themselves, with different textures, can spawn with zombie horsemen, can burn in sunlight; this page covers zombie horse as they appeared and behaved before the update.

== Spawning ==
Zombie horses could not spawn naturally. Zombie horses could be spawned by the {{cmd|summon}} command or with their spawn egg. When using a spawn egg, 20% spawn as baby zombie horses.

== Drops ==
=== On death ===
{{DropTable
|{{DropLine|name=Rotten Flesh|quantity=0-2|lootingquantity=0-1|notes=until}}
|notes=
}}
* {{xp|1|3}} when killed by a player or [[tamed wolf]].
If saddled, they dropped their saddle.

Baby zombie horses yielded neither items nor experience.

== Behavior ==
Zombie horses behaved like normal horses, roaming idly and occasionally stopping to rear, swish their tails, or lower their heads as though eating the grass. Unlike [[sheep]] and like other horses, the eating animation does not cause any grass to be consumed. Like other undead mobs, zombie horses can breathe in water.

Zombie horses have a {{frac|1|900}} chance to regenerate {{hp|1}} health point each game tick.{{verify|edition=bedrock}}

{{IN|bedrock}}, zombie horses could be killed by the [[conduit]] like any regular hostile mob and prevent the player from sleeping.

Zombie horses can safely fall 6 blocks before they begin to accumulate [[Damage#Fall damage|fall damage]], unlike most mobs, which can only safely fall 3 blocks. Zombie horses also take half of the normal fall damage (rounded up) that most other mobs take ({{hp|0.5}} damage per block over the safe distance instead of {{hp|1}}). If an entity is riding a zombie horse, they receive the same fall damage as the zombie horse.

Zombie horses cannot be [[bred]].

If a player pulling a zombie horse with a lead enters a [[boat]], the zombie horse follows by swimming the same speed as the boat. A player riding a zombie horse is dragged along if the zombie horse is being pulled by a lead, and can even be lifted up in the air in the same way.

=== Mob type ===
Being an [[undead]] mob, they are: 
* Damaged by the status effect [[Instant Health]] and healed by the status effect [[Instant Damage]].
* Unaffected by the status effects [[Regeneration]] and [[Poison]].
* Ignored by the [[wither]].
* Affected by the [[Smite]] enchantment.
* A threat to [[armadillo]]s, causing them to hide in their shell.

Unlike most other undead mobs, zombie horses did not burn in direct sunlight. They are protected from burning when wearing horse armor, similar to other undead mobs wearing [[helmet]]s. As horse armor has no [[durability]], it does not break from absorbing the damage like helmets do.

=== Taming ===
Zombie horses could not be tamed normally. However, {{in|java}}, with the use of commands, a zombie horse could be [[tamed]] in the same way as a regular horse, granting the [[Best Friends Forever]] advancement: {{cmd|1=ride @s mount @e[type=minecraft:zombie_horse,limit=1]}}. A tamed zombie horse could also be summoned using {{cmd|summon minecraft:zombie_horse ~ ~ ~ <nowiki>{Tame:1b}</nowiki>}}.

=== Statistics ===
Zombie horses were spawned with constant health of 25, speed ranging from 0.1125 to 0.3375, and jump strength ranging from 0.4 to 1.0, with the variable statistics trending toward their averages of 0.225 and 0.7 respectively.

== Usage ==
{{see also|Transportation}}
{{exclusive|java|section=1}}
Tamed and [[saddle]]d zombie horses could be used as a means of [[transportation]]. They could also be used to climb hills and jump fences.

{{IN|bedrock}}, they could not be ridden.

=== Equipment ===
[[File:Zombie Horse GUI.png|thumb|right|GUI of a zombie horse.]]

Tamed zombie horses have a lone equipment slot:
* {{SlotSprite|Saddle}} Saddle slot: For equipping a [[saddle]]

{{IN|bedrock}}, they have no slot.

Baby zombie horses cannot be equipped, as they cannot be ridden.

Equipment can be placed on a zombie horse by holding it and then {{control|using}} on the zombie horse, or by accessing its inventory. A zombie horse’s inventory can be accessed by mounting the horse and using {{control|inventory}} control, or by {{control|sneaking}} and then {{control|using}} or pressing the "open inventory" button{{only|bedrock}} on the zombie horse. The player cannot open the inventory on an untamed zombie horse, though they can open containers.

=== Riding ===
{{main|Riding}}

Once a zombie horse was tamed and saddled, the player could control it with directional controls, {{control|jump}}, and the mouse. The player dismounted using the {{control|dismount}} control. Like when riding other entities, it was possible for a player to use a [[nether portal]] or [[end portal]] while on a zombie horse.

A ridden zombie horse automatically ran up any one-block-high slope. The horse and rider could safely fit through a space as low as 2.75 blocks high. Lower clearance risks suffocating the rider if the rider's head enters a non-transparent block. The zombie horse itself could enter gaps as low as 1.625 blocks high, but might itself take suffocation damage when clearance is less than 1.75 blocks. Like other horses, zombie horses could not fit through a 1-block-wide gap.

Ridden zombie horses had the ability of "jump charging". When the player rode them, the experience bar on the [[HUD]] is replaced by a jump charging bar. The zombie horse was then able to {{control|jump}}, and holding the control charges for a higher leap. Zombie horses were not affected by the [[Jump Boost]] effect.
